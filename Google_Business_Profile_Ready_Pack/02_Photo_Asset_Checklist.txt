PHOTO RULES
- Use real, recent and well-lit images.
- Do not heavily filter or misrepresent the clinic.
- Remove visible patient records, prescriptions and confidential information.
- Obtain permission for every identifiable staff member or patient.
- Keep original files; do not send screenshots of photographs.
- Avoid adding promotional text, phone numbers or excessive graphics to photos.

VERIFICATION PRIVACY
Google may offer video, phone, email, mail or another method. Use only the method
shown in the profile. Do not upload private documents publicly as profile photos.
Never send verification codes to an unknown third party.
