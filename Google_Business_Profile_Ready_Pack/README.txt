GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE - READY PACK

Purpose
-------
This folder prepares everything needed to create, claim and launch the doctor's
Google Business Profile. Complete the files only with truthful, real-world
information supplied and approved by the doctor.

Files
-----
01_Profile_Data.txt           Master profile information sheet
02_Photo_Asset_Checklist.txt  Required photos and file naming guide
03_Content_Templates.txt      Description, services, posts and reply templates
04_Launch_Runbook.txt         Creation, verification and post-launch procedure

What can be prepared now
------------------------
- Data fields and approval workflow
- Photo list, dimensions and file naming
- Business description and service templates
- Review and question-response templates
- Verification-day checklist
- Post-launch quality checks

What cannot be completed in advance
-----------------------------------
- The doctor's real profile information
- Profile creation/claiming under the doctor's Google account
- Google's verification method and approval
- Genuine clinic photographs and evidence
- Genuine patient reviews
- Guaranteed placement or ranking in Google Search

Ownership rule
--------------
The doctor/client should be the Primary Owner. The developer should be invited
as a Manager or Owner where appropriate. Never request or store passwords, OTPs,
recovery codes or identity documents in this folder.
